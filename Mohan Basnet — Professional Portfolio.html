A little about me

Teaching with purpose. Building with vision.

My professional journey combines classroom teaching with hands-on experience running a local computer and tuition institute. I value continuous learning, responsible leadership, and work that creates lasting value for people.

I continue to explore sustainable business ideas and ways to contribute to Nepal’s development.
